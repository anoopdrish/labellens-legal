LabelLens

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

LabelLens is a mobile app that reads food labels and photos of meals and explains what is in them. It is run by Anoop Krishnan, an individual developer based in Bangalore, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers. Your photos are analysed and then discarded; we keep the results. You can delete your scan history or your whole account inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Email addressTo create your account and send you a one-time sign-in code. We don't send marketing email.
Photos you take or choosePhotos of food labels or meals are sent to our servers and to our AI provider only to analyse them. We do not store the photos after the analysis is done.
Scan and meal resultsThe product name, nutrition values, grade, ingredients flagged and suggestions from each scan, and the foods and portions from each meal photo, so you can see your history.
Food logFoods you choose to "Add to today", with their calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat.
Health profile (optional)Goals and conditions you choose (for example weight loss, diabetes, high blood pressure, pregnancy), your diet and your allergies. Used to give a personal verdict on each scan.
Body details for targets (optional)Sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal, used to work out your daily calorie and macro targets.
Subscription statusWhether you have LabelLens Pro, and how many free scans you have used.

Your health profile and body details are health-related information. You choose whether to enter them, and the app works without them. We use them only to personalise your results and targets.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing history, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: sign you in, analyse your photos, show your results, history and daily log.
  • To personalise verdicts and calorie targets if you have entered a profile.
  • To manage the free scan allowance and LabelLens Pro.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems (for example, short-lived server logs of errors and how long a scan took).

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: hosting, database and sign-in. Data is stored in Supabase's Mumbai (India) region.
  • Anthropic: the AI that reads your photos. Each photo, with your health profile if you set one, is sent to Anthropic's API to produce the analysis. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers the one-time sign-in code to your email address.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store and RevenueCat: process LabelLens Pro subscriptions. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Brandfetch: provides brand logos. The app loads a logo image by the brand's website name; Brandfetch sees a normal web request from your device but no personal information from us.

Shopping buttons (BigBasket, Blinkit, Instamart, Amazon) and WhatsApp sharing open those services, whose own privacy policies apply. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can delete your scan history from the Profile screen, remove items from your daily log, or delete your account from Profile → Delete my account. Deleting your account permanently removes your account, scans, meals, food log, profile and targets from our database. Service providers may keep short-lived logs for security for a limited period.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security so each account can only read its own data. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your information in the app at any time. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, to correct or erase it, or to withdraw consent to processing of your health profile (which you can also do by clearing it in the app). If you are in India, you have these rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India. We aim to reply within 30 days.

7. Children

LabelLens is meant for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Not medical advice

LabelLens gives general nutrition information and estimates. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about any health condition, diet or weight-loss plan.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
