Delete your account

LabelLens · labelllens@gmail.com

In the app (fastest)

Open LabelLens → Profile → Delete my account → confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email labelllens@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my LabelLens account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account and email address, all scan and meal results, your food log, health profile, calorie targets and free-scan count. Nothing is kept afterwards, except short-lived security logs held by our hosting provider for up to 30 days.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a LabelLens Pro subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions (or in your Apple ID settings on iPhone).
